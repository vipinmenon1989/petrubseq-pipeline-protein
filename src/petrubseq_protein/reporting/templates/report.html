6. Perturbation effects

Downstream analyses on the processed object. Perturbed cells = single-guide targeting cells of one target; controls = single-guide non-targeting cells; ambiguous and multi-guide cells are excluded. Perturbation target = the gene the guide acts on; response genes = genes that change downstream; gene program (P) = a cluster of co-responding genes; perturbation module (M) = a cluster of perturbations with similar responses. Associations below are correlations, not evidence of mediation or causality; methods and references in docs/PERTURBATION_EFFECTS.md.
